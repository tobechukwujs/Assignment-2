What is Backend Development?

Backend development is like the engine of a car - you don't see it, but nothing works without it.

When you visit a website and log in, post a comment, or buy something online, the backend is doing all the work behind the scenes. It's the part of a website or app that:

  • Stores and manages data in databases
  • Processes user requests and sends back responses
  • Handles security and authentication
  • Connects different parts of the application together
  • Makes sure everything runs smoothly and efficiently

Think of it this way: if a restaurant is a website, the backend is the kitchen where all the food is prepared. The frontend is the dining area where customers see and enjoy their meals. Backend developers are the chefs making sure everything is cooked perfectly and served on time.
